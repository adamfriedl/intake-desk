Title: Disputing Errors on Credit Reports (FTC Public Education)

Your credit report affects whether you can get a loan, a job, insurance, or an apartment. It is important that the information in your report is complete and accurate.

You are entitled to a free copy of your credit report every 12 months from each of the three nationwide credit reporting companies. You can request reports through the official centralized service. Review reports from all three companies because they may contain different information.

If you find mistakes — wrong accounts, incorrect balances, accounts that are not yours, or outdated negative information — you can dispute them with the credit reporting company and with the company that furnished the information.

To dispute an error, write to the credit reporting company and explain what is wrong. Include copies (not originals) of documents that support your position. Identify each item you dispute, state the facts, and ask that the error be corrected. Send your letter by certified mail and keep a copy.

The credit reporting company must investigate most disputes within 30 days unless it considers your dispute frivolous. It must forward your dispute to the furnisher, which must investigate and report back. When the investigation is complete, the credit reporting company must give you the results in writing and a free copy of your report if the dispute changes the report.

If the furnisher continues to report incorrect information, you may need to dispute again or seek advocate help. Negative information generally may stay on a credit report for a limited number of years depending on the type of entry, but inaccurate information should be corrected or removed.

Checking your own report is a "soft inquiry" and does not lower your score. Disputing errors is separate from responding to a debt collection lawsuit — court deadlines still apply if you are sued.

This excerpt supports credit-reporting intake questions. It does not guarantee a particular score change or timeline for every dispute.
