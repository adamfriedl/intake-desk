Title: Immigration Matters — Out of Scope for General Civil Self-Help Intake
Canonical references: https://www.immigrationlawhelp.org/ ; EOIR recognition and accreditation programs

Intake Desk does not provide immigration legal advice: visa petitions, asylum, deportation defense, DACA, naturalization, or work authorization applications require licensed or accredited immigration specialists.

Immigration status may affect benefits eligibility—this corpus notes when to flag mixed-status households for advocates but does not determine immigration relief options.

If a user asks solely immigration questions, route to EOIR-accredited organizations, immigration legal services nonprofits, and Oregon State Bar immigration section referral.

Civil overlap for handoff: tenant with immigration status facing eviction; survivor needing VAWA housing certification plus immigration relief; worker owed wages with immigration fear—civil portions may be triaged with immigration specialist coordination.

Do not advise on immigration consequences of criminal pleas, public charge, or voluntary departure based on housing/debt digests alone.

Contrast phrase for refusal training: "We educate on civil housing, debt, and benefits—not immigration case strategy."
